- 2026-07-19 — "Are we fully on Svelte 5?" is two questions, not one. - 2026-07-19 — A Svelte 5 bottom-sheet drawer opened but slid OFF-SCREEN (played its close animation on open): the custom `attrTransition` toggled `data-starting-style` for `direction === "in"` and `data-ending-style` for everything else, but a bidirectional `transition:fn` directive is invoked by Svelte 5 with `direction: "both"` on intro — which fell into the exit branch and applied the ending style on OPEN. Better behavior: inspect the working tree, generated artifacts, browser behavior, and dependency payload before accepting. Proof: independent review caught and removed client-side Shiki/WASM and restored modal accessibility before commit.

Proof: the review distinguishes transitions that truly interpolate from state changes that mount instantly or unmount before slowed motion completes.
- Workload leveling: Failure pattern: launching more concurrent reviewers than the account can execute creates gaps and wasted retries. Better behavior: cap parallel work at the observed concurrency ceiling and retain a local evidence path for rejected lanes. Proof: every claimed domain has direct evidence even when a delegated reviewer cannot start.
