## Design Context

### Users
FreeTheAi serves developers, students, roleplay users, coding-agent users, and other builders worldwide. They need to understand the free OpenAI-compatible service quickly, obtain a Discord API key, configure their chosen client, and reach a successful first request without billing or avoidable friction. No audience should be privileged at the expense of another; paths should remain legible by intent and client.

### Brand Personality
Precise, independent, and generous. The interface should inspire confidence that the service is technically credible and operationally transparent while retaining the project's distinctive community-built character.

### Aesthetic Direction
Flagship-quality dark interface with restrained skeuomorphic depth, monochrome surfaces, warm orange emphasis, editorial typography, and purposeful dither texture. Preserve the established visual identity and responsive bottom navigation. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, oversized rounding, excessive cards, decorative copy, and motion without informational value.

### Design Principles
1. Make the next useful action obvious for both new and returning users.
2. Preserve one clear hierarchy: identity, task, evidence, action.
3. Use dither, depth, color, and motion only to communicate state or reinforce brand.
4. Keep every path accessible by keyboard, touch, reduced motion, and narrow screens.
5. Prefer shared Feature-Sliced primitives and the smallest complete vertical change over page-specific duplication.
